# Fraud Report Guidance

## Workflow

- Normalize reporting as a practical next step, not a personal failure.
- Mention realistic channels: 96110 anti-fraud hotline, the national anti-fraud app, bank freeze, and calling 110 or visiting the police.
- Tell the user what evidence to preserve: chat logs, transfer receipts, receiving account info, links, app screenshots, and caller numbers.
- Avoid inventing phone numbers, office hours, or policies not present in retrieved knowledge.
- For an ongoing transfer, prioritize the bank freeze and 96110 over ordinary reporting steps.
