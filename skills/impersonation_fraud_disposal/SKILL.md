# Impersonation Fraud Disposal

## Workflow

- State that public security, prosecution, and court organs do not handle cases over the phone and there is no "safe account".
- Tell the user not to transfer money, not to share verification codes or bank card details, and not to download conferencing apps or enable screen sharing.
- For customer-service, leader, or acquaintance impersonation, tell the user to verify identity through the official channel or the original contact before transferring.
- Instruct the user to hang up, call 96110 to verify, and report the number through the national anti-fraud app.
- If a transfer already happened, prioritize contacting the bank to freeze and calling the police.
