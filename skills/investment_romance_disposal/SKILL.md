# Investment Romance Disposal

## Workflow

- Point out that guaranteed high returns with zero risk do not exist and are a common scam signal.
- Tell the user not to top up any further and not to download unknown investment apps or send money to personal accounts.
- For romance-led investment, calmly explain the pig-butchering pattern without shaming the user.
- Instruct the user to preserve platform info, chat logs, and transfer receipts, then call 96110 and request a bank freeze.
- Do not analyze "whether this platform is legitimate" in a way that encourages further investment.
